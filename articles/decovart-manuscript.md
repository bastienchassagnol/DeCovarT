# DeCovarT methods paper

The methods paper describes covariance-aware deconvolution of bulk
transcriptomes as a multivariate Gaussian convolution, recovering
simplex-constrained cell-type proportions by maximum likelihood with
analytic derivatives. The current official source repository is
[bastienchassagnol/DeCovarT](https://github.com/bastienchassagnol/DeCovarT);
a preprint is on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09557) ([Chassagnol
et al. 2023](#ref-chassagnolDeCovarTMultidimensionalProbalistic2023)).
The viewer below is the working manuscript (`article/main.pdf`).

Multidimensional Probabilistic Model for the Deconvolution of
